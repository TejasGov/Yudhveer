Your swings turn toward the nearest enemy and step in to close the gap. Once a swing has landed, a slide, a deflect or the next blow can cut its follow-through short. Deflect just as a blow lands to throw the attacker off balance. Break an enemy's posture and your blows land more than twice as hard while it recovers.

About the author

Tejas Govind (TejasGov)

Hi, I'm Tejas Govind (TejasGov). I made YUDHVEER: the story, the combat, the levels and the direction, and I voice Yudhveer myself. It's an action game rooted in Indian culture, inspired by the games I've loved since I was a child: Tekken, Mortal Kombat, God of War, Elden Ring and many more. Claude Opus 5.5 is what made it possible for me to pull this off. It runs straight in your browser, with no download and no install, it's built with Three.js, Rapier and TypeScript, and it's free and open source.

The project on GitHub: github.com/TejasGov/Yudhveer
